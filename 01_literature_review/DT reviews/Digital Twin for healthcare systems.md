## Brief Overview
A review without any specifications. The overall sense is that DT4H will revolutionize healthcare with examples from:
- Enhancing patient care
- Predictive analytics and preventive interventions
- Optimization of clinical operations
- Training and simulation
Although, separation DT4H technology through these sections are valuable and gives insight into classification of DT4H. In general, it's just a bridge to specific, cited papers, which could contain more specific metrics, proves or examples.

## Key takeoffs
- An idea, which supports that DT4H are not just one component, but rather several with different responsibilities and applications. We don't need to compete between ML approaches and physics-based mathematical ones, but first we can set as a predictive component of future states and second one as a baseline of patient state.
- Digital twin definition - "A digital replication of living or non-living physical entities."
- technologies for DT can be categorized into two groups:
	- data-driven statistical
	- integration of multi-scale knowledge and data into a mechanical model
- An idea to run a project about digital twin simulation for medical students practice
