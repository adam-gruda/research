## Brief Overview
A framework fo type 2 diabetes, which integrates knowledge graphs, multiomic data and mechanistic models. The framework are created from 3 components - ml, knowledge graphs and mechanistic models. First one is responsible for forecasting disease progression. KG is used to explain predictive features identified by machine learning in terms of mechanistic or casual relationship between them.They assist in feature selection to ML. Mechanistic models can serve as a digital representations of individual patients, but paper focuses rather on first two components.
